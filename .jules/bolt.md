## 2024-06-28 - Caching Filament Stats Dashboards\n**Learning:** Filament dashboard pages calculating multiple aggregate metrics (like sums and counts over 30 days) execute heavy sequential scans on every page load.\n**Action:** Use `Cache::remember` with a 60-second TTL to memoize these expensive operations in memory, avoiding redundant database lookups while maintaining near-real-time accuracy.
